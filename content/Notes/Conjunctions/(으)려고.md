## verb/adjective stem + (으)려고
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**in order to...**

Used to indicate intention and purpose of an action

(으) for verb or adjective stems that end in a consonant other than ㄹ

## Examples
<details>
<summary>예뻐 보이<b>려고</b> 화장을 해요</summary>
<div class="context">(I) wear makeup in order to look pretty.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>우리 아빠는 살을 빼<b>려고</b> 저녁을 안 먹어요.</summary>
<div class="context">My dad doesn't have dinner in order to lose weight.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>토니 씨는 면접하<b>려고</b> 양복을 샀어요</summary>
<div class="context">Tony bought a suit for the interview.</div>
</details>
