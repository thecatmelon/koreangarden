## verb + adjective stem + (으)ㄹ 터니까

**because (something will happen)**

- A combination of (으)ㄹ 터이다 (show speakers will/guess) and 니까 (to show a reason)
- the subject of the two sentences should be different
## Examples
<details>
<summary>사람들이 많이 <b>올 테니까</b> 음식을 많이 준비하세요.</summary>
<div class="context">Many people will come, so please prepare a lot of food </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>초대장은 제가 보<b>낼 테니까</b> 걱정하지 마세요</summary>
<div class="context">I will send the invitation, so please don't worry.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>제가 수저를 준비<b>할 테니까</b> 반찬을 가져오세요.</summary>
<div class="context">I will set the chopsticks and the spoons, so please take the rice and side dishes. </div>
</details>
