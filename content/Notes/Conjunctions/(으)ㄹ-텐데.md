## verb/adjective stem + (으)ㄹ 텐데

**Two uses:**
- when used to <u>connect</u> two sentences: *although something will happen*
- when used at the <u>end</u> of the sentence: shows speaker's guess

sentence structure is a combination of (으)ㄹ 터이다 (show speakers will/guess) and ㄴ데
## Examples
<details>
<summary>장례식에 <b>갈 텐데</b> 검은색 정장이 없어요.</summary>
<div class="context">I am going to a funeral, but I don't have a black suit. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>내일은 약속이 있<b>을 텐데</b> 모레 만나면 안 되까요?</summary>
<div class="context">I have an appointment tomorrow. Can we meet the day after?</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>지하철로 가면 더 <b>빠를 텐데.</b></summary>
<div class="context">It would be faster if we go by subway.</div>
</details>
