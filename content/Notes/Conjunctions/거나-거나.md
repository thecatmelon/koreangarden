## verb/adjective stem A + 거나 + verb/adjective stem B + 거나

- *no matter A or B*
- used to include two opposite situations
## Examples
<details>
<summary>그 사람이 <b>오거나 말거나</b> 상관없어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Whether that person is coming or not is none of my business.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>잘생겼거나 못생겼거나</b> 인품이 중요해요.</summary>
<div class="context">N matter if a person is good looking or ugly, the personality is important. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>비가 <b>오거나 눈이 오거나</b> 제때 등교합니다.</summary>
<div class="context">(I) go to school on time no matter it rains or snow.</div>
</details>
