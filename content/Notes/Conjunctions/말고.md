## 말고
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**Not A, but B; not only A, but also B**

- the exact meaning depends on context
- when following a verb, it should be **verb stem + 지 말고**  and be translated as "don't do, but do"

Can also be thought of as **instead** ([Migaku - 말고](https://migaku.com/grammar/korean/malgo-instead))
## Examples

<details>
<summary>여드름이 나면 짜<b>지 말고</b> 약을 바르세요</summary>
<div class="context">If you got acne, dont press, please apply medicine. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 저녁에 고기<b>말고</b> 야채만 먹었어요. </summary>
<div class="context">(I) didn't eat meat, but only vegetables for dinner </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>한국에서 여성 회장품<b>말고</b> 남성 회장품도 많아요. </summary>
<div class="context">Besides female makeup, there are many male makeups in Korea </div>
</details>
