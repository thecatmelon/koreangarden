## verb/adjective stem + (으)려면
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**(if one wants to...)**

use (으) if the verb/adjective stem ends in a consonant other than ㄹ
## Examples
<details>
<summary> 건강해지<b>려면</b> 밤을 새우찌 말아야죠. </summary>
<div class="context">If you want to be healthy, of course you should not stay up all night. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>살을 빼<b>려면</b> 꾸준히 운동해야죠.</summary>
<div class="context">If you want to lose weight, of course you need to keep exercising. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>예뻐 보이<b>려면</b> 항상 웃어야죠.</summary>
<div class="context">If you want to look pretty, of course you should smile a lot. </div>
</details>
