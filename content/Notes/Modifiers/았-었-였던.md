## verb stem/adjective stem + 았/었/였던 + noun
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Memory*

**had done...**

this is a noun modifier ending to recall a completely finished event

## Examples

<details>
<summary>유학 <b>갔던</b> 치구들이 다 돌아 왔어요. </summary>
<div class="context">My friends who went to study abroad all came back.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>전에 <b>만났던</b> 사람인데 이름이 기억이 안 나요. </summary>
<div class="context">(That) is a person I have met before, but I can't recall their name.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 제가 <b>갔던</b> 나라 중에 이탈리 아기 제일 좋아요 </summary>
<div class="context">Among all the countries I've been to, I like Italy the most. </div>
</details>
