## verb stem/adjective stem + 았/었/였던 + noun
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Memory*

**had done...**

this is a noun modifier ending to recall a completely finished event

## Examples
제가 **갔던** 나라 중에 이탈리 아기 제일 좋아요<br>Among all the countries I've been to, I like Italy the most.

전에 **만났던** 사람인데 이름이 기억이 안 나요.<br>(That) is a person I have met before, but I can't recall their name.

유학 **갔던** 치구들이 다 돌아 왔어요.<br>My friends who went to study abroad all came back.
