## verb/adjective stem + 던 + noun
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Memory*

**used to...**

던 is a noun modifier ending to recall habitual behaviour or event that happened in the pase

## Examples

<details>
<summary> 그때 매일 듣<b>던</b> 노래가 기억나요. </summary>
<div class="context">Do you remember the song you used to listen to everyday at that time?</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>이건 제가 쓰<b>던</b> 일기항이에요</summary>
<div class="context">This is the diary I used to write on</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>어렸을 때 살<b>던</b> 도시에 가 보고 싶어요</summary>
<div class="context">I want to go see the town I used to live when I was a child</div>
</details>
