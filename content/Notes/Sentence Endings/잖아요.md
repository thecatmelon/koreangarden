## verb/adjective stem + 잖아요

**you should know that...; ...and you know it**

- states a reason or a fact that listener knows well 
## Examples
<details>
<summary>날씨가 춥<b>잖아요</b></summary>
<div class="context">You should know that the weather is cold.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 매일 바쁘<b>잖아요</b></summary>
<div class="context">Everyday is busy and you know it. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>내일은 토요일이<b>잖아요</b></summary>
<div class="context">You should know that tomorrow is Saturday. </div>
</details>
