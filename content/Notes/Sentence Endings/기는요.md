## verb/adjective stem + 기는요
**(not...)**
- mainly used in conversation as a <u>response</u> to express <u>an opposite opinion</u> OR to <u>show modesty to a praise</u>
## Examples
<details>
<summary>잘하<b>기는요</b>. 아직 멀었어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Not really. I'm still far (from being good).</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 비싸<b>기는요</b>. 가짜예요.</summary>
<div class="context">It's not expensive. It's fake. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>고맙<b>기는요</b>. 친구잖아요.</summary>
<div class="context">Don't thank me. You should know that we are friends. </div>
</details>
