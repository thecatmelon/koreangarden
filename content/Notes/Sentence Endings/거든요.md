## verb/adjective stem + 거든요

**because...**

- provides a reason OR emphasizes an argument
## Examples
<details>
<summary>한국어를 열심히 공부하고 있어요. 통역사가 되고 싶<b>거든요</b>.</summary>
<div class="context">I study Korean very hard, because I want to become an interpreter. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>인터넷에서 사면 훨씬 더 싸<b>거든요</b></summary>
<div class="context">Because it's much cheaper if you buy it from the internet. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>친구라ㅏㅇ 약속이 있어요. 내일은 내 생일이<b>거든요</b></summary>
<div class="context">I have an appointment with my friends. Because tomorrow is my birthday. </div>
</details>
