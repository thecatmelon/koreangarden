## verb/adjective stem + (으)ㄹ걸 (그랬다)

**It would be better if...; I should have...**

- shows regret of something done
## Examples
<details>
<summary> 면세점에서 살 <b>걸 그랬어요.</b></summary>
<div class="context">I should have bought (it) from the duty free shop.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 어제 일찍 잘 <b>걸 그랬어.</b></summary>
<div class="context">I should have slept earlier </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>일찍 공항에 도착할<b>걸 그랬어요</b></summary>
<div class="context">It would be better if I arrived at the airport earlier </div>
</details>
