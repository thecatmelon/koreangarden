## verb/adjective stem + (으)ㄴ/는가 보다

**I guess...**

- used to show speakers best guess on experiences or facts.
- *can* be used with 이다

### Conjugation

|                                                         |         |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| verb stem, 있다.없다 stem, tense prefinal ending -았/었/였, -겠 | - 는가 보다 |
| 이 다                                                     | -ㄴ가 보다  |
| adjective stems ended with a vowel or ㄹ                 | -ㄴ가 보다  |
| adjective stems ending with a consonant other than ㄹ    | -은가 보다  |

## Examples
<details>
<summary>저것은 김 선생님으 오토바이 인<b>가 봐요</b></summary>
<div class="context">I guess that over there is teacher Kim's motorcycle. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>물건을 잃은 사람이 많은<b>가 봐요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I guess a lot of people lose their things. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>이 가방은 비싼<b>가 봐요</b></summary>
<div class="context">I guess this bag is expensive. </div>
</details>
