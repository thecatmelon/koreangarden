## verb stem + 기 시작하다
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Festival*

**start doing...**

Uses 기 which turns verb into noun (link back at some point)
## Examples
<details>
<summary>3월부터 여러 군데에서 봄꽃 축제가 열리<b>기 시작합니다.</b> </summary>
<div class="context">In March, the Spring Flower Festival will begin in various places</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 1월부터 준비하<b>기 시작했습니다.</b></summary>
<div class="context">(We) started preparing in January </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>2월에 홍보하<b>기 시작했습니다.</b> </summary>
<div class="context">(We) started promoting in February. </div>
</details>
