## verb/adjective stem + 던 + noun
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Memory*

**used to...**

던 is a noun modifier ending to recall habitual behaviour or event that happened in the pase

## Examples

이건 제가 쓰**던** 일기항이에요<br>This is the diary I used to write on

어렸을 때 살**던** 도시에 가 보고 싶어요<br>I want to go see the town I used to live when I was a child

그때 매일 듣**던** 노래가 기언 나요.<br>Do you remember the song you used to listen to everyday at that time?
