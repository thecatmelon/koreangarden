## verb/adjective stem + 듯(이)

**as if...**

- used to make a metaphor
- shows that what happens in second sentence is almost the same as first sentence

<u>Personal note</u>
- 듯(이) for sentence comparison
- 처럼 for noun comparison

## Examples
<details>
<summary>지금 보<b>듯(이)</b> 상황이 좋지 않아요</summary>
<div class="context">As you can see now, the situation is not good </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>비 오<b>듯(이)</b> 땀이 나요</summary>
<div class="context">I was sweating like it's raining </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>돈을 물 쓰<b>듯(이)</b> 싸요.</summary>
<div class="context">I spend money like using water. </div>
</details>
