## verb/adjective stem + (으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 만큼

**as much as...; to the extent ...**

- indicates the degree of the second sentence is similar to that of the first sentence
## Examples
<details>
<summary>음식은 먹<b>을 만금</b> 가져가세요.</summary>
<div class="context">Take the food as much as you can eat. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>노롁하<b>는 만금</b> 좋은 결과가 올 거예요.</summary>
<div class="context">Things will be good if one tries as hard as you.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>놀 만금</b> 놀았어요.</summary>
<div class="context">(I) have played enough </div>
</details>
