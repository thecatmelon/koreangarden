## verb/adjective stem + (으)려고
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**in order to...**

Used to indicate intention and purpose of an action

(으) for verb or adjective stems that end in a consonant other than ㄹ

**Examples**
예뻐 보이**려고** 화장을 해요
(I) wear makeup in order to look pretty.

토니 씨는 면접하**려고** 양복을 샀어요
Tony bought a suit for the interview.

우리 아빠는 살을 빼**려고** 저녁을 안 먹어요.
My dad doesn't have dinner in order to lose weight.
