## noun에 따르면/에 의하면

**According to...**
## Examples
<details>
<summary> 뉴스<b>에 따르면</b> 사람들이 환경 오염에 걱정이 많습니다.</summary>
<div class="context">According to the news, people are concerned about the environment pollution </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>일기예보<b>에 따르면</b> 오늘 비가 올 거예요</summary>
<div class="context">According to the weather forecast, it will rain today </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>정부<b>에 의하면</b> 대기오염이 점점 시해지고 있습니다.</summary>
<div class="context">According to the government, the air pollution is getting more severe. </div>
</details>
