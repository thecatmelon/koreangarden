## In English
- ex: "the cake was eaten by the poppy"
- "be+past participle" forms a passive sentence
- Passive voice: "by zombies"

## In Korean
- two ways to make passive verb:
	- 하다 verbs = 되다 verbs
	- add 이, 히, 리, 기 after verb stem

## Examples

| General verb    | Passive verb        |
| --------------- | ------------------- |
| 시작하다<br>start   | 시작되다<br>be started  |
| 예약하다<br>reserve | 예약되다<br>be reserved |
| 선택하다<br>select  | 선택되다<br>be selected |
| 이용하다<br>use     | 이용되다<br>be used     |
| 보다<br>see       | 보이다<br>be seen      |
| 쓰다<br>use       | 쓰이다<br>be used      |
| 쌓다<br>pile      | 쌓이다<br>be piled     |
| 섞다<br>mix       | 섞이다<br>be mixed     |

## The usage of passive verb
- SPECIFIC NOTE ON PARTICLES: generally the subject (가/이) is the one who does the action and the object (을/를) receives the action
- in passive voice, the subject is the one who has the action done to them.
	- as a result, particle usage is particular

**Example
- Normal verb: 이 자리**를** 예약**했**습니다 -> (someone) reserved this seat.
- Passive verb: 이 자리**가** 예약**됐**습니다 -> this seat is reserved (by someone).

- Sometimes sentences with a passive verb is not translated into a passive sentence in English
	- Normal verb: 새 학기**를** 시작**했**습니다.
	- Passive verb: 새 학기**가** 시작새 학기를 시작했습니다습니다
- Both sentences mean "the new semester begins", but the second uses passive verb which means you have to pay attention to the particle used (passive = subject not object)
