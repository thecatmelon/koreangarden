## noun + (이)라면서요

**I heard that ... is that true?**

Used to solicit confirmation. Conjugation is the same as indirect quotation

## examples
이분이 안내원**이라면서요**?<br>I heard that this is the tour guide, is that true?

이게 네잎클로버**라면서요**?<br>I heard that this is the four leaf clover. Is that true?
