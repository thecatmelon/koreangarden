## noun + 탓에

(due to...)

- usually followed by a negative result
## Examples
<details>
<summary>심한 다이어트 **탓에** 일하다가 쓰러졌어요</summary>
<div class="context">I passed out during work due to the restricted diet. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>누나는 급한 성격 **탓에** 친구와 싸움을 자주 합니다.</summary>
<div class="context">(my) elder sister always argues with her friends due to her hot temper. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>흡연 **탓에** 암에 걸렸어요.</summary>
<div class="context">(I) got cancer due to smoking </div>
</details>
