## verb stem/있다 or 없다 stem/았-었-였 + 나 보다

**I guess...**

- shows speakers guess based on experience or facts
- cannot be used with 이다
## Examples
<details>
<summary>미나 씨가 신분증을 잃어버렸<b>나 봐요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I guess Mina has lost her ID. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>미나 씨는 집 열쇠가 어디 있는지 모르<b>나 봐요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I guess Mina doesn't know where the key to her house is. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>토니 씨으 안경은 집에 있<b>나 봐요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I guess Tony left his glasses at home </div>
</details>
