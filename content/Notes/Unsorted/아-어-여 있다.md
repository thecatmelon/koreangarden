## verb stem + 아/어/여 있다
- indicates status continues
- usually used with [[passive-verbs]]
## Examples
<details>
<summary> 할머니께서는 소파에 <b>앉아 계세요. </b></summary>
<div class="context">(My) grandmother is sitting on the sofa.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>문이 <b>열려 있어요.</b></summary>
<div class="context">The gate is open </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>서랍이 <b>잠겨 있어요.</b></summary>
<div class="context">The drawer is locked. </div>
</details>
