## noun + 덕분에
**because ...; thanks to ...**

is usually followed by a positive result
## Examples
<details>
<summary>외할머니 **덕분에** 저녁 잘 먹었어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Thanks to (my) maternal grandmother, I had a good dinner. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>사촌 결혼 **덕분에** 규욕에 다녀 왔어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Thanks to (my) cousin's wedding, I went to New York </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>삼촌 **덕분에** 이 일은 잘 해결됐어요</summary>
<div class="context">Thanks to (my) uncle, this thing got solved perfectly </div>
</details>
