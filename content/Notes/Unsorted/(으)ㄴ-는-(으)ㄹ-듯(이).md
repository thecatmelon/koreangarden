## verb/adjective stem + (으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ 듯(이)

**as if...**

- when [[듯(이)]] is by itself, each sentence can be treated as equivalent
- when (으)ㄴ/는/(으)ㄹ is added, the first sentence is an assumption based on the <u>fact</u> stated in the second sentence
## Examples
<details>
<summary>가슴이 <b>터질 듯이</b> 아파요.</summary>
<div class="context">(My) heart hurts as if it will blow up.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>그 사람은 기분이 <b>좋은 듯이</b> 콧노래를 볼러어요.</summary>
<div class="context">That person is humming as if they are happy. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>이 집은 <b>그린 듯이</b> 아름다워요.</summary>
<div class="context">This house is beautiful as if it is in a painting. </div>
</details>
