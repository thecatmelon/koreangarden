## (으)므로

**Since...**

- introduces a reason
- mainly used in written form
- cannot follow imperative (command/request) or propositive (proposals or suggestions)
## Examples
<details>
<summary> 이 길은 쓰레기가 많<b>으므로</b> 냄새가 납니다.</summary>
<div class="context">Because there are many trashes on the street, it is very stinky. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>오염이 심하<b>므로</b> 기침하는 사람이 많아지고 있습니다.</summary>
<div class="context">Because the pollution is severe, more and more people are suffering from cough.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>근차에는 길이 복잡하<b>므로</b> 조심하게 운전하셔야 합니다. </summary>
<div class="context">Since the road here is complicated, please drive carefully. </div>
</details>
