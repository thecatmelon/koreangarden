## verb stem + 아/어/여 버리다

- indicates the finish of an action (in a negative way)
- some verbs contain 아/어/여 버리다, therefore no space required before 버리다
	- 잃어버리다 (to lose)
## Examples
<details>
<summary>아이가 과자를 <b>먹어 버렸어요</b></summary>
<div class="context">The kid ate all the snacks. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>오늘 지하철에서 지갑을 <b>잃어버렸어요</b></summary>
<div class="context">(I) lost my wallet on the subway today</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>돈을 다 <b>써 버렸어요.</b></summary>
<div class="context">(I) spent all the money </div>
</details>

## Some thoughts...
Seems like this is a subjective way of describing an objective fact. In English, we use tone of voice to indicate this, but in Korean we can use this sentence pattern.
