## 아무
- generally used with negative sentence
- when used individually, it is an indefinite pronoun meaning "anyone"

## Examples
<details>
<summary><b>아무</b>도 안 왔어요</summary>
<div class="context">No one came. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>여기 <b>아무</b>도 없어요.</summary>
<div class="context">There is no one here. </div>
</details>

## Other uses
- can be used as indefinite article that modifies noun. Examples:
	- 아무것 = anything
	- 아무 말 = any word
	- 아무 생각 = any thought

## More examples
