## 말고
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**Not A, but B; not only A, but also B**

- the exact meaning depends on context
- when following a verb, it should be **verb stem + 지 말고**  and be translated as "don't do, but do"

**Examples**
- 저녁에 고기**말고** 야채만 먹었어요.
  (I) didn't eat meat, but only vegetables for dinner
  
- 한국에서 여성 회장품**말고** 남성 회장품도 많아요.
  Besides female makeup, there are many male makeups in Korea
  
- 여드름이 나면 짜**지 말고** 약을 바르세요
  If you got acne, dont press, please apply medicine.
