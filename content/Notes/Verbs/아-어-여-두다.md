## verb stem + 아/어/여 두다
- used to indicate the state caused by the action continues
## Examples
<details>
<summary>인스턴트 식품을 많이 <b>사 뒀어요.</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I have bought a lot of instant foods. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>오워 <b>두세요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">Please keep it memorized </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 식탁 위에 놓<b>아 두세요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">Please put it on the table.</div>
</details>
