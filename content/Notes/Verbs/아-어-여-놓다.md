## verb stem + 아/어/여 놓다

- used to indicate the state continues after the action is done

## Examples
<details>
<summary>호텔을 예약<b>해 놓았어요</b>.</summary>
<div class="context">(I) have booked a hotel.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>아들의 생일을 위해 케이크를 <b>만들어 놓았어요.</b> </summary>
<div class="context">(I) have made a cake for (my) son's birthday.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 외출 준비를 <b>해 놓았어요.</b> </summary>
<div class="context">(I) have done the perparation for going out.</div>
</details>
