## verb/adjective stem + (으)려면
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**(if one wants to...)**

use (으) if the verb/adjective stem ends in a consonant other than ㄹ
## Examples

살을 빼**려면** 꾸준히 운동해야죠.
If you want to lose weight, of course you need to keep exercising.

예뻐 보이**려면** 항상 웃어야죠.
If you want to look pretty, of course you should smile a lot.

건강해지**려면** 밤을 새우찌 말아야죠.
If you want to be healthy, of course you should not stay up all night.
