## verb stem + 자마자/자
**once/as soon as ..., ...**

- describes an action that happens right after another action
## Examples
<details>
<summary>방에 들어<b>자마자/자</b> 에어컨을 켰어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Once (I) entered the room, (I) turned on the air conditioner. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>공항에 도찬하<b>자마자/자</b> 체크인했어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Once (I) arrived at the airport, I checked in. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>승무원은 좌석에 앉<b>자</b> 좌석 벨리를 맸어요.</summary>
<div class="context">Once the crew member sat on the seat, he/she fastened the seat belt.</div>
</details>
