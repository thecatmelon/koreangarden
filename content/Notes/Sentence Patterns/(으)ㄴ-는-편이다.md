## verb/adjective stem + (으)ㄴ/는 편이다
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**be the type that...; belong to the side that...**

- talks about characteristic of subject
- grammar pattern uses 편 (side) to categorize the subject into certain group

## Examples:
제니 쌰는 날씬한 **편이에요**
Jenny is slim
(literal translation: Jenny belongs to the slim side)
  
토니 씨는 남자 중에 머리가 긴 **편이에요**.
Tony has long hair for a guy
(literal translation: Tony is on the side of having long hair for a guy)

저는 물만 마셔도 살이 찌는 **편이에요**.
I gain weight easily
(literal translation: I'm the type that gains weight even from drinking water.)
