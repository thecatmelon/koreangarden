## verb stem + 기 바라다
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Festival*

**hope that...**

Uses 기 which turns verb into noun (link back at some point)
## Examples
<details>
<summary>잘 살<b>기 바래요</b>. </summary>
<div class="context">I hope you live well. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>행복하<b>기 바래요</b>.</summary>
<div class="context">I hope that you are happy. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>재미있게 놀<b>기 바래요</b>.</summary>
<div class="context">(We) hope (everyone) has a good time.</div>
</details>
