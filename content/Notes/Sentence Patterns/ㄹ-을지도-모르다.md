## verb/adjective stem ㄹ/을지도 모르다

**it may/might...**

expresses a guess with high level of uncertainty
## Examples
<details>
<summary>2050년에 모든 집안일은 로봇이 다 할 수 있을<b>지도 몰라요.</b></summary>
<div class="context">Translation </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>나중에 노트북은 종이처럼 <b>앏을을지도 몰라요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">Laptops might be as thin as paper in the future </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>나중에 스마트폰보다 더 편리한 핸드폰이 생길<b>지도 몰라요</b></summary>
<div class="context">Maybe a cell phone that is more convenient than smart phone will come out in the future. </div>
</details>
