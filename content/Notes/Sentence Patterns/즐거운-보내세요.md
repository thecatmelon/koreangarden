## 즐거운 + name of the festival + 보내세요
*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Festival*

**Happy ___ I wish you a happy ____**

The general expression used in holidays and festivals
## Examples
<details>
<summary><b>즐거운</b> 성탄절 <b>보내세요</b></summary>
<div class="context">I wish you a Merry Christmas! </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> <b>즐거운</b> 명절 <b>보내세요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I wish you a happy festival! </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> <b>즐거운</b> 추석 <b>보내세요</b> </summary>
<div class="context">I wish you a happy Chuseok! </div>
</details>
