## verb/adjective stem <u>indicating change</u> 아/어/여 가다/오다
- shows *direction* of an action/change
- if direction *moves away* from subject, or goes from *current to future*, use 가다
- if direction *moves toward* from subject, or goes from *past to current*, use 오다
- **adjective must contain meaning of change**. Otherwise use 아/어/여지다 to change adjective into a changing adjective
