## 촌수 = degree of kinship
- system that counts the degree of relationship between two family words
- 촌 = unit to count blood relation
- 수 = the number

## Examples
- relationship between husband and wife is closest, 0 촌
- parent and children = 1 촌

## More specific example
- Relationship between Mina and her parents is 1촌
- Mina and her grandparents is 2 촌
- Mina and her siblings is also 2촌
- Mina and her aunts/uncles is 3촌
- Mina and her cousins is 4촌
