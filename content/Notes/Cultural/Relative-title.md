- unlike in English, family members in Korean have different words depending on mother or father's side

- add **외** when referring to maternal grandparents
- use **고모** for father's sisters
- use **이모** for mother's sisters
- father's elder brothers are **큰아버지** or **삼촌**
- mother's brothers, no matter the age, are **삼촌**
	- although relation between person and their aunt is also 3촌 we cannot call aunts 삼촌
- adding terms like 오빠, 누나, after 사촌 indicates gender and relative age of cousin
	- **사촌오빠** = elder male cousin to a female
	- **사촌여동생** = younger female cousin
