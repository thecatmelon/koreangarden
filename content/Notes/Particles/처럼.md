## noun + 처럼

**like...**

used to make comparison with other objects

## examples

<details>
<summary>저는 새<b>처럼</b> 날고 싶어요. </summary>
<div class="context">I want to fly like a bird </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 오늘은 봄<b>처럼</b> 따뜻해요. </summary>
<div class="context">Today is as warm as spring.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>잎이 비<b>처럼</b> 떨어지고 있어요. </summary>
<div class="context">Leaves are falling down like rain.</div>
</details>
