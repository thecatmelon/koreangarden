## noun + 처럼

**like...**

used to make comparison with other objects

## examples
오늘은 봄**처럼** 따뜻해요.<br>Today is as warm as spring.

잎이 비**처럼** 떨어지고 있어요.<br>Leaves are falling down like rain.

저는 새**처럼** 날고 싶어요.<br>I want to fly like a bird
