## 던 vs 았/었/였던

*reference: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Memory Extended Reading*

던 and 았/었/였던 are mostly interchangeable. But 았/었/였던 can only be used if followed by a sentence with past tense

Right: 유학 **갔던** 친구들이 다 <u>둘아왔어요</u>
Wrong: 유학 **가던** 친구들이 다 <u>둘아왔어요</u>
