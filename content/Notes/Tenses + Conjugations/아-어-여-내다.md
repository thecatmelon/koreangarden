## verb stem + 아/어/여 내다
- used to indicate the finish of an action (in a positive way)
- some verbs contain 아/어/여내다, which means there is no space required before 내다.
	- 해내다 (to achieve)
	- 찾아내다 (to find out)
	- 알아내다 (to find/figure out)
## Examples
<details>
<summary>그녀의 이름을 꼭 <b>알아낼 거야</b>.</summary>
<div class="context">(I) will find out her name.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>드디어 <b>해냈어요 </b></summary>
<div class="context">(I) finally made it</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>슬픔을 <b>이겨 낼거야</b>. </summary>
<div class="context">(you) will overcome (your) grief. </div>
</details>
## some thoughts copied from [[아-어-여-버리다]]
Seems like this is a subjective way of describing an objective fact. In English, we use tone of voice to indicate this, but in Korean we can use this sentence pattern.
