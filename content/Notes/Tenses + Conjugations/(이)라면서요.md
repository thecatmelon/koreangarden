## noun + (이)라면서요

**I heard that ... is that true?**

Used to solicit confirmation. Conjugation is the same as indirect quotation

## examples

<details>
<summary> 이분이 안내원<b>이라면서요</b>?</summary>
<div class="context">I heard that this is the tour guide, is that true?</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>이게 네잎클로버<b>라면서요?</b></summary>
<div class="context">I heard that this is the four leaf clover. Is that true?  </div>
</details>
