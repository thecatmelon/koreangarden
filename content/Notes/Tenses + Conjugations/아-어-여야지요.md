## verb stem + 아/어/여야지요

*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**Of course someone should...**

indicates the decision of the speaker, or used to recommend other people to do something

## Examples:

꾸준히 운동해**야죠**<br>Of course (you) need to keep exercising.

항상 웃어**야죠**<br>Of course (you) should smile a lot.

밤을 새우지 말아**야죠**<br>Of course (you) should not stay up all night
