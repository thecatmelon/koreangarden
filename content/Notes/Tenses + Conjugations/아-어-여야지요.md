## verb stem + 아/어/여야지요

*resource: Lingodeer Korean 2 - Appearance*

**Of course someone should...**

indicates the decision of the speaker, or used to recommend other people to do something

## Examples:

<details>
<summary>항상 웃어<b>야죠</b> </summary>
<div class="context">Of course (you) should smile a lot. </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 꾸준히 운동해<b>야죠</b> </summary>
<div class="context">Of course (you) need to keep exercising.</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>밤을 새우지 말아<b>야죠</b> </summary>
<div class="context">Of course (you) should not stay up all night </div>
</details>
