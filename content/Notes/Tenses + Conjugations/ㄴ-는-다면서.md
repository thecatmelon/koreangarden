## verb/adjective stem + (ㄴ/는)다면서
#reference/lingodeer/korean2/picnic 

**I heard that ... is that true?**

Solicits confirmation. Conjugation is the same as conjugation of indirect quotation (link back at some point)

## examples

<details>
<summary>여기 볒꽃이 유명하<b>다면서요</b>? </summary>
<div class="context">I heard that the cherry blossom trees here are famous, is that true?</div>
</details>

<details>
<summary>아이들이 소풍을 좋아<b>한다면서요</b>? </summary>
<div class="context">I heard that children like picnics, is that true? </div>
</details>

<details>
<summary> 도시락을 <b>쌌다면서요</b>?</summary>
<div class="context">I heard that you put together some lunch boxes, is that true? </div>
</details>

### quick review of conjugation of indirect quotation
<u>Verbs:</u> 
*end with vowel stem*: stem + ㄴ다고
*end with a consonant stem*: 는다고

<u>Adjectives, 있다/없다, 았/었/였/겠</u>:
stem + 다고
